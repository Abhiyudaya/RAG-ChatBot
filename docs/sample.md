# Nimbus Cloud Platform — Internal FAQ

## Accounts and access

To reset your password, go to the Nimbus login page and click "Forgot
password". A reset link is sent to your registered email and is valid for 30
minutes. If the link expires, request a new one. Password resets are not
available over the phone for security reasons.

New employees are provisioned a Nimbus account within one business day of
their start date. If you cannot log in after your first day, contact the IT
help desk at ext. 4500.

## Billing

Nimbus bills monthly on the first of each month. Usage is metered per
gigabyte of storage and per compute-hour. The Starter plan includes 100 GB of
storage and 50 compute-hours; overage is charged at 0.12 USD per GB and 0.08
USD per compute-hour. Invoices are available under Account > Billing and are
issued as PDF documents.

Annual plans receive a 15 percent discount compared to paying monthly.

## Support tiers

Standard support responds within one business day. Priority support, included
with Enterprise plans, responds within two hours during business hours.
Critical production incidents on Enterprise plans have a one-hour response
target, 24 hours a day.

## Data retention

Deleted files are moved to Trash and kept for 30 days before permanent
deletion. Backups of active projects are retained for 90 days. Audit logs are
retained for one year and are available to account administrators only.
